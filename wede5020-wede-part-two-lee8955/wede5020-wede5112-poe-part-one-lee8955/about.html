About Bright Futures

Rooted in Alexandra, growing futures across Johannesburg.

Our Mission

To break the cycle of poverty by providing quality educational support, mentorship and safe spaces for children aged 5-18 in Alexandra township.

Our Vision

Every child in Alexandra finishes school with confidence, skills and opportunity for tertiary education or employment.

Our History

Founded in 2015 by former teacher Nomvula Khumalo, Bright Futures started under a tree with 12 learners. Today we operate from a converted community hall serving over 300 children with 50 volunteers.
