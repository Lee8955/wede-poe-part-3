Our Programmes

Free education support for 300+ children in Alexandra.

After-School Tutoring

Ages 8-18. Maths, English, Science. Monday to Thursday 15:00-17:30. 50 volunteer tutors from University of Johannesburg.

Cost: Free

School-Readiness Programme

Ages 5-7. Early literacy, numeracy and social skills. Prepares children for Grade 1. 90 percent improvement rate.

Cost: Free

Mentorship and Life Skills

Ages 12-18. Career guidance, study skills, gender-based violence awareness, peer support circles.

Cost: Free

Holiday Bootcamps

Intensive revision during school holidays for Grade 10-12 learners. Exam prep and university applications.

Cost: Free

Parent Workshops

Monthly workshops helping parents support learning at home, nutrition and positive discipline.

Cost: Free

Feeding Scheme

Daily nutritious meal for all learners attending programmes. Supported by local donors.

Cost: Free
